Before you begin

Upload only the original perfume image first. This is the image the tool will edit. Image editing happens outside Scentkeep. The 720 × 720 size is a suggested artwork convention, not an app requirement. Check the exported dimensions, transparency and label text; a prompt alone does not guarantee them.

Product image prompt

Copy the complete prompt without changing its measurements.

Extract only the perfume bottle from the provided image and transform it into a high-quality, professional e-commerce product image.

1. Background Removal

- Completely remove the existing background, including any shadows, decorative elements, props, and surrounding objects.
- Export the bottle with a fully transparent background (alpha channel).
- Ensure clean, precise edges without white halos, leftover background pixels, or jagged outlines.

2. Image Enhancement

- Improve sharpness, clarity, and overall image quality.
- Adjust brightness, contrast, and exposure for balanced, professional studio lighting.
- Enhance realistic reflections on the glass and cap.
- Preserve the bottle's original colors, proportions, shape, branding, and label text.
- Remove unwanted glare, noise, and compression artifacts without making the bottle look artificial.

3. Standardized Sizing

- Set the final image dimensions to exactly 720 × 720 pixels.
- Resize the extracted bottle proportionally to approximately 600 pixels tall within the 720 × 720 canvas.
- If a wide bottle would exceed the canvas, scale it down to preserve at least 60 pixels of padding on every side.
- Center the bottle horizontally and vertically.
- Maintain its original aspect ratio without stretching, cropping, or distortion.
- Leave consistent transparent padding around the bottle.
- Ensure all perfume bottles appear approximately the same height when displayed together in a product grid, regardless of their original image dimensions.

4. Export Requirements

- Format: PNG
- Resolution: 720 × 720 px
- Background: 100% transparent
- Quality: High-resolution, sharp, clean product photography
- Alignment: Centered
- No added text, borders, watermarks, artificial drop shadows, or decorative backgrounds.

Final result: A clean, premium-quality perfume bottle PNG with transparent surroundings, consistent visual height, and standardized dimensions, ready to use in a perfume collection website with white product cards.

To use the result, host the PNG at an accessible image URL and paste that URL into the fragrance record’s Image URL field. Scentkeep does not upload a local PNG from this guide.
