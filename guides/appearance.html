Home/Appearance settings

Make yourself at home.

Choose a colour palette and a light or dark appearance for your desktop journal.

Appearance and profile preferences are saved on this installation.

On this page
  1. Choose your colours
  2. Switch light and dark
  3. Where preferences are kept
  4. Your profile

Choose your colours

  1. Open Settings

    Find the Appearance section above Automatic backups.

  2. Select a palette

    Choose one of the named palette buttons. The selected palette is applied immediately and its button is marked as selected.

The v0.2.9 release includes 11 palettes:

  • Porcelain Blue
  • Candy pop
  • Velvet Dusk
  • Wildwood
  • Tropical Sorbet
  • Amber Glow
  • Crimson Ember
  • Scarlet Bloom
  • Citrus Breeze
  • Miami Pop
  • Velvet Saffron

Switch light and dark

Use the Dark mode switch in Appearance to turn dark appearance on or off. Your selected palette stays selected when you change the mode.

Before you choose a mode, Scentkeep follows your system’s light or dark preference. Using the switch saves an explicit light or dark choice for later launches.

Where preferences are kept

Palette and appearance choices are stored locally and restored when the app opens again. If local storage is unavailable, the selected appearance still applies for the current session.

These preferences are separate from your fragrance and note CSV files. Set up the appearance you want on each installation; WebDAV collection sync does not transfer it.

Add your name and picture

Select the avatar in the top bar to open Edit profile. Enter Your name, paste a Profile picture URL and choose Use link to preview it. Choose Save profile to apply the changes, or Cancel to leave the saved profile as it was.

Remove picture clears the image and returns to an initial-based avatar. Your profile is stored locally on this installation and is separate from your Parfumo account and CSV backups.
