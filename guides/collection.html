Home/Collection guide

Organize your collection.

Keep owned bottles, future finds and finished fragrances easy to browse.

Use shelves for status, filters for quick views and search for names, brands or collection names.

On this page
  1. Add a fragrance
  2. Choose a shelf
  3. Find a record
  4. Prepare bottle artwork
  5. Edit or delete

Add a fragrance

  1. Choose Add fragrance

    Open it from the sidebar or the collection toolbar.

  2. Enter the identifying details

    Add the fragrance name, brand and release year (1800–2100), then any optional collection, concentration or perfumer information.

  3. Save the record

    The fragrance appears on the shelf selected in its Status field.

Prefer a starting point? Use Find on Parfumo in the editor to review and import available fragrance details, then finish the record yourself.

Import a fragrance from Parfumo ↗

Choose the right shelf

  1. Collection

    Use this for fragrances you currently own.

  2. Wishlist

    Keep fragrances you are considering, including an optional current price.

  3. Empties

    Retain finished bottles as part of your history.

Search and filter

Search matches fragrance names, brands and collection names. On Collection, All includes all shelves; Owned narrows the view to bottles on the Collection shelf. Wishlist and Empties show their matching records. The Filters button toggles Owned and All, while the layout control switches between grid and list views.

Results appear in pages of 12. Use Previous and Next below the grid or list; changing a search, shelf or filter returns to the first page.

Use the sidebar arrow to collapse or expand the desktop navigation. On a narrower window, use the navigation menu button in the top bar to open the drawer, then choose a shelf or page.

Edit or remove a record

Open the ··· actions button on a fragrance card and choose Edit or Delete. You can also open the fragrance and choose Edit fragrance. Changes to its shelf, bottle counts or favourite status are made in the editor.

Prepare bottle artwork

Consistent transparent bottle images make the collection grid easier to scan. Use the recommended image prompt to remove the background, keep a consistent scale and export a centered 720 × 720 pixel PNG.
