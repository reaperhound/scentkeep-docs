Home/Import a fragrance from Parfumo

A head start on every record.

Search Parfumo by fragrance or brand, or paste a fragrance link to bring available details into your journal.

Review the preview and the form before saving. You can still enter everything manually.

  2. Find the fragrance

Before you begin

Open Settings and connect your Parfumo account, then save the session. Return to Add fragrance, or edit an existing record, and stay on the General information step.

Connect your Parfumo account ↗

Find the fragrance

  1. Use Find on Parfumo

    Enter at least two characters of a fragrance or brand name, or paste its Parfumo fragrance URL. Choose Search Parfumo.

  2. Open a result

    Choose a result marked Review to read the fragrance details. A direct fragrance link opens its preview without the result list.

  3. Check what is available

    Review the identity, release year, concentration, audience, perfumer, description and note layers. Read any warnings about missing data.

Review and save

  1. Choose Use these details

    Available identity fields and non-empty note layers replace their corresponding values in the form. Missing fields remain as they were.

  2. Complete the three steps

    Review General information, Fragrance pyramid and Additional details. Correct missing values and set your own shelf, bottle counts, prices and ratings.

  3. Save the fragrance

    The preview does not save a record. Choose Save fragrance, or Save changes when editing. Imported notes and available images, categories and source links are saved to the note library at this point.

Images and sources

Images remain linked to Parfumo. The fragrance description keeps a Source link. Check the image and note details before saving, especially when updating an existing record.

If the lookup fails

Check your saved Parfumo session in Settings. You can refresh it by opening Parfumo, signing in and saving the session again. If search has no results, try a direct fragrance link. For verification problems, follow the connection troubleshooting steps, or continue entering the record manually.
