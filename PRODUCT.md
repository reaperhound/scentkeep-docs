## Users
Fragrance collectors who want to catalogue their bottles, wishlist and empties, remember scent details, and keep control of their collection records.

## Product Purpose
The public website explains the Scentkeep desktop fragrance journal, links to its GitHub downloads, and provides eleven practical guides. The desktop application lives in the sibling `../scentkeep` repository; this project remains a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

WebDAV merges matching records with remote values taking precedence; deletions do not propagate. Parfumo may require Cloudflare verification. Do not imply mobile support, automatic recognition, encryption or conflict-aware live sync. Preserve all guide content and functional download destinations. No invented customer counts, testimonials, prices or endorsements.
